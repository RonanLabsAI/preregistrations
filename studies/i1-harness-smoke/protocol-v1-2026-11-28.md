# I1 harness smoke v1: one own-vLLM research unit end to end, with a mid-run claim revocation

Product: `i1-harness-smoke` (operational smoke of the Idleworks research queue; not a scientific study).
Frozen: 2026-11-28 (PT). Nothing has run under this protocol yet.
Status: preregistered; execution follows the public commit of this file.

## Question

Can the Idleworks queue run one research unit end to end on a DGX Spark, using the unit's own vLLM server, and survive a revocation of its fleet claim mid-run without losing or duplicating work?

This is an infrastructure check. Model accuracy is reported for completeness only. It decides nothing and is not a claim about the model.

## Public inputs (frozen, hashed)

- `harness/prompts.jsonl`: 40 two-number addition questions (3-digit operands, generated once with seed 20261128). SHA-256 `7cf31ee9002fd7e0435fb8a1eb83d6c06882e5135a88ea9cacc64a255dade162`.
- `harness/smoke_job.py`: the job. It sends each prompt once (temperature 0, seed 0, at most 32 output tokens, thinking off), appends one line per item to `receipts/results.jsonl` and skips items already present, so a resumed unit continues where it stopped. It sleeps 1.5 s between items so the revocation test has a window. SHA-256 `4e12bb6a18f448386f2311ad4d78184d199ab361753c1418bba964bae7f9e97a`.

## Arm, unit and environment

One arm (`spark`), seed 1, unit `sparksmoke-1`. A second arm (`rtx6000`, unit `rtxsmoke-1`) may be added by a dated amendment when an RTX PRO 6000 is free. It uses the same inputs and pass criteria.

- Hardware: one DGX Spark (GB10), driver 580.142, sharing the GPU with standing resident embedding and chat models. The unit must not evict them.
- Engine: the unit's own vLLM container, image `vllm/vllm-openai@sha256:3af6a45b060d681aa6254200f87859d1bb3c1f962afb3d15540b4b31d4a41063` (vLLM 0.30.1rc1.dev245, torch 2.13.0+cu130), serve arguments `--max-model-len 4096 --gpu-memory-utilization 0.12 --enforce-eager`.
- Model: `Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B`, `model.safetensors` SHA-256 `f47f71177f32bcd101b7573ec9171e6a57f4f4d31148d38e382306f42996874b`.
- Environment fingerprint (frozen): `gb10|drv580.142|vllm0.30.1rc1.dev245@3af6a45b060d|qwen3-0.6b@f47f71177f32|ctx4096|gmu0.12|eager|job@4e12bb6a18f4`.
- Never used: the production LLM router or the research router. The job calls the unit's own loopback server only.

## Procedure

1. The queue takes its preemptible fleet claim on the Spark, checks memory headroom (at least 20 GiB available), starts the unit's container pinned to the Spark GPU and runs the job.
2. After at least 5 and before 35 items are recorded, the operator files a foreign fleet claim that covers the Spark. The queue must stop the unit, remove its container and release its own claim.
3. The operator releases the foreign claim. The queue must take a new claim and resume the unit, and the job must skip the items already recorded.
4. The unit completes and the queue validates its result package.

## Pass criteria (all required)

- P1: `results.jsonl` holds each of the 40 ids exactly once, with items from at least two attempts.
- P2: after the foreign claim, the unit is reported interrupted (not failed), and its container is gone within 5 minutes (the declared release bound).
- P3: no container from this unit remains after completion, and the queue's own claim is released once the queue is idle.
- P4: the result package passes `rl validate package`.
- P5: resident models on the Spark are still loaded afterwards.

Accuracy (correct / 40) is reported with its count and is not a criterion.

## Budget, kill criteria and interruption contract

- Budget: 1 GPU-hour.
- Kill criteria: stop on any eviction of a resident model, any call to a production or research router, or headroom below the floor.
- Interruption contract: maximum release 5 minutes; maximum lost work one item (each item is written as it completes); revocable claim yes.

## Outcome

The outcome, pass or fail per criterion, will be recorded in `outcome-<date>.md` in this directory.
