# Preregistration policy

Stephen approves a product card or its standing template before execution. Approval does not waive scientific or operational admission gates. New work needs a frozen protocol committed publicly before execution; its result manifest records the exact protocol SHA-256 and immutable public commit URL. Preserve each frozen version and use a dated amendment for changes.

Protocols specify question, source claim, arms, public inputs and hashes, seeds, metrics, denominator and confidence-interval method, budget, kill criteria, interruption contract, hardware and environment fingerprint. Use a preemptible fleet claim; production takes priority. Verify a real smoke and runnable command before admission. Held drafts remain held.

Historical work without an externally verifiable prior timestamp carries: "Preregistered locally; timestamps not externally verifiable." Never manufacture a prospective timestamp for historical results.

Every preregistered run outcome, null or not, is recorded publicly here. Posts and papers cover meaningful nulls only: outcomes that change a decision or claim, refute a public claim, or teach something reusable. The PI decides the publication tier. An unrun disposition is not a measured null.

Validate cards and result packages before drafting or exporting. Audit every public artifact for private content; automated scanning supplements source review. No patient records, proprietary datasets, private infrastructure details, credentials or model weights are permitted.

This repository does not authorize posting, submission, email, or deployment. Result publication beyond these approved preregistration and outcome records requires the PI's approval.
